# Brand candidates

**Status:** Unapproved. Preliminary screening only. No formal trademark, company-name, social-handle, or app-store clearance has been performed.
**Domain target:** `esimprove.com` (ownership currently unverified — see Phase 0 report).

## Selection criteria

1. Reads naturally as a travel eSIM brand.
2. Connects clearly to the letters in `esimprove.com`.
3. Distinct from competitors and not descriptive enough to be unprotectable.
4. Works on a mobile app icon, in a receipt, and in support email.
5. Does not claim coverage, speed, ratings, or a partnership that does not exist.
6. Can be pronounced in English without a forced explanation.

## Candidate 1 — eSIMprove (recommended)

**Why it fits:** `eSIM` + `prove` is exactly the string in `esimprove.com`. It signals proof of connectivity and trust, and it makes the domain read as the brand without needing a second domain.

**Positioning:** “Travel connected. Proven.”
**Risks:** It is category-descriptive; trademark protection may be narrow. It could be confused with a testing tool. A formal clearance search is required.

**Preliminary web check:** exact-phrase searches did not produce a clear matching eSIM brand, but search-engine results are not a trademark register and are inconclusive. No conclusion of availability is claimed.

## Candidate 2 — Improva

**Why it fits:** A short, invented mark derived from “improve” that can extend beyond eSIM into travel connectivity. Easier to protect than a descriptive category name. The relationship to `esimprove.com` is still audible.

**Positioning:** “Better connection, by design.”
**Risks:** Less immediately understandable as travel eSIM. Pronunciation and spelling may need reinforcement. Search returned unrelated results, not a clearance result.

## Candidate 3 — RoamProof

**Why it fits:** “Roam” makes the travel use case obvious; “Proof” supports a trust-first positioning and a clean visual identity. Better as a consumer brand than a pure descriptive name.

**Positioning:** “Proof you’re covered.”
**Risks:** Does not mirror the domain as closely as eSIMprove. The name may suggest coverage guarantees we cannot make unless backed by verified supplier terms. “Proof” must not become a false reliability claim.

## Recommendation

Choose **eSIMprove** if the domain is confirmed and the owner wants the closest domain-to-brand fit. Choose **Improva** if a more protectable, expandable brand matters more than immediate category recognition. Choose **RoamProof** if the brand should lead with travel and reliability rather than the SIM category.

No logo, typeface, colour palette, social handle, or trademark filing is final. Visual identity work starts only after the owner picks a name and confirms domain control.

## Domain and name checks still required

- Confirm registrar ownership of `esimprove.com`; public checks currently show no registration/DNS.
- Commission a trademark search in the intended markets and classes (at minimum class 9, 38, and 42; get professional advice).
- Check company-name registers in the jurisdiction of incorporation.
- Check app-store names, social handles, and email local parts.
- Check whether a confusingly similar mark exists in travel eSIM or telecom.
- Consider defensive registrations only after the owner approves spend.
- Do not use a name in public marketing until clearance is documented.

## Naming rules for the UI

- Use `eSIM` with that capitalisation in body text where it is a category reference.
- If eSIMprove is selected, use `eSIMprove` consistently in product copy, but do not force a capitalisation that breaks accessibility or search.
- Never write “the best”, “No. 1”, “guaranteed coverage”, “5G everywhere”, or invented review scores.
- The legal footer must show verified legal entity details only after they are supplied.
